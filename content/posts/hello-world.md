Hello, world. Humans and agents alike, welcome to my personal blog.

I intend to write about software engineering in this new age: one in which few
engineers still doubt that AI can produce decent code, and many already use it
every day. Plenty of other things are happening in the world, too, but I hope
to keep this blog free of political opinion—while packing it with technical
ones.

A little about me: I played a small part in the cloud-native shift. I was an
early contributor to Kubernetes, helped build the first version of `kubeadm`,
and was an early advocate for GitOps—writing and speaking about it before the
term was widely used. I also created `eksctl`.

I have worked at cloud-native startups including Weaveworks and Isovalent, and
then work on platform engineering and supply-chain security at Docker. Today,
I build internal delivery tooling at CoreWeave.
